Drag across the rail

D — the literal request, built honestly. Real hangers on a real rail: an orthographic camera, 1px `LineBasicMaterial` (which is the house hairline, so the 3D reads as the same drawing), and a damped angular spring per hanger that takes an impulse from the pointer. It pauses off-screen and on a hidden tab, caps device pixel ratio at 2, and the flat SVG underneath is what ships when WebGL or the CDN is missing. Costs, stated plainly: about 170KB of three.js over Egyptian mobile data on a screen whose meaning is "we failed"; a GPU context on a mid-range Android at full brightness; and motion that conveys no state, which `DESIGN.md` bans. It is the best-looking option and the one the design system argues against.
